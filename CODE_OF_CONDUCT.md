# Code of Conduct

## Inclusivity
All are welcome to contribute to MyPaySU regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Politics
While MyPaySU is a project with political themes, discussion of real-world politics is not permitted on GitHub. This project is for technical development and discussion only.

## Conduct
You must not be actively hostile towards other contributors. We do not require unnecessary 'niceness', but interactions must remain focused on the project and the code. Technical disagreement is fine; personal hostility is not.
